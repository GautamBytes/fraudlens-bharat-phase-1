# Synthetic Data Prompt Templates

Use these templates to expand the Phase 1 dataset. Generated examples must be reviewed manually before being added.

## KYC Scam

Generate 20 fake KYC scam SMS messages in Hinglish. Use common Indian banking/wallet context, urgent tone, abbreviations like "plz", "ur", "acct", and slight misspellings. Include some examples with suspicious links and some with phone numbers. Do not use real bank URLs or real phone numbers.

## Digital Arrest

Generate 20 fake digital arrest WhatsApp messages in Hinglish. The scammer should impersonate police, customs, cyber cell, CBI, or court officials. Include threat language, secrecy instructions, fake case IDs, and pressure for video call/payment. Keep grammar imperfect.

## Fake Job

Generate 20 fake job scam messages in Hinglish/English. Include part-time work, Telegram HR, registration fee, joining kit fee, daily earning promise, and unrealistic salary. Avoid real company names unless generic.

## Investment Scam

Generate 20 fake investment scam messages in Hinglish. Include guaranteed returns, crypto/trading groups, VIP tips, doubling money, and urgency to deposit today. Add mixed Hindi/English phrasing and fake UPI IDs.

## Loan Scam

Generate 20 fake loan scam messages. Include instant approval, low interest, processing fee, CIBIL claims, app download links, and repayment threats. Use Hinglish and SMS style.

## Courier Scam

Generate 20 fake courier/customs scam messages. Include blocked parcel, illegal item accusation, FedEx/DHL-style language without real identifiers, fake case number, and urgent verification.

## UPI Refund Scam

Generate 20 fake UPI refund/cashback scam messages. Include collect request, refund pending, cashback claim, "enter UPI PIN to receive", and QR/payment link language.

## OTP Phishing

Generate 20 fake OTP phishing messages. Include login alerts, OTP requests, card/PIN/CVV requests, account takeover urgency, and fake support messages. Mix English, Hindi, and Hinglish.

## Anti-Overfitting Instruction

For every category, vary:

- Message length
- Use of Hindi words
- Spelling mistakes
- Whether a URL is present
- Whether a phone number or UPI ID is present
- Threat vs reward tone
- SMS vs WhatsApp style
